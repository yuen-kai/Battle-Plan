Battle Plan
1-SHEET PITCH

PLATFORM: PC, Mobile
TARGET AUDIENCE: Teenagers - Young Adults (12-24)
GENRE:  Positional strategy, auto-battler


SUMMARY:  It's a positional strategy game between two players. They simultaneously plan the movement of their team’s characters on a grid. After locking in, the round plays out automatically: units move and shoot after they stop. Players can activate abilities during the execution phase.


GAME FEATURES: 
* Planning Phase – Both players drag movement paths for each unit
* Execution Phase
   * All units move together
   * Shoot after stopping
   * Players can activate character’s abilities in real time
      * Opponent is given a chance to counter ability or tell troops to dive out of the way
* Goals
   * Basic mode: team deathmatch - kill all enemy units
   * Additional modes: Control point, escape mode, protect the president, etc
* Many different characters each with different roles


UNIQUE SELLING POINTS (USPs)
* Unique gameplay


COMPETING PRODUCTS
* Frozen synapse but a lot more simple and with special abilities 
* XCOM but multiplayer and simultaneous  
* Valorant but you control the big-picture movement not the characters themselves
* Chess but moving multiple characters and simultaneous. Characters also shoot and have abilities

Two players simultaneously plan movement paths for their 3 units and then the units follow those paths and automatically shoot each other after stopping. During the round a player can activate a unit’s ability and the opposing player is given a chance to move their units out of the way. A player wins if they eliminate all of the opponent’s units first.

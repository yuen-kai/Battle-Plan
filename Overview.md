Two players simultaneously plan movement paths for their five-unit crews and then the units follow those paths and automatically shoot each other after stopping. During the planning phase a player can toggle between movement and activating a unit’s ability and lock orders in early. Locked orders can be unlocked and edited while the planning window remains open; unlocked orders submit automatically when the shared timer expires. If they choose ability, the opposing player is given a chance to move their units out of the way (if applicable).

Elimination is won by defeating the opposing crew. In King of the Hill, a central 3×4 block of twelve cells forms a control zone; a team wins by holding sole control at the end of three consecutive rounds. Eliminated units remain dead in both modes. Eliminating the entire opposing crew wins immediately; if both crews are wiped in the same round, the match is a draw.

**Current rules.** Every ability starts ready and enters a server-authored cooldown only after a valid post-dodge activation. Pogo, Ram Charge, Shield Wall, Smoke Screen, Suppressing Fire, Bunker Buster, Arc Surge, Triple Volley, and Shatter Leap recharge after 2 full rounds; Grenade after 3; Area Lock after 4. Death does not reset or pause cooldowns. A crew is exactly **five** characters drawn from the eligible roster; repeats are allowed, so the same character can fill more than one slot. The Commander is available with Smoke Screen.

**Naming note.** The former "Shotgunner" is now **Ramrod** — a name was needed once a second shotgun-wielder existed. The shield/pistol character is **Sentinel**. **Salvo**, **Breach**, **Voltaic**, **Farsight**, **Blitz**, and **Outrider** are the machine-gunner, rocket-launcher, electric, magic-archer, aggressive-jumper, and mobile-rifle characters from the same batch — all working names, drafted by Claude, pending the designer's approval. See `docs/design/DesignerVerbatim.md` for the designer's direction.

**Damage budget.** The new characters are tuned to the original five's per-magazine damage band (36–50; Ramrod's 80 is the spread-weapon outlier). Current per-magazine figures: Salvo 45, Breach 45, Voltaic 44, Farsight 45, Outrider 44, and Blitz 40. Each new ability also ends with a per-ability **recovery wait** before its weapon comes back; the original five have none.

Two players simultaneously plan movement paths for their 3 units and then the units follow those paths and automatically shoot each other after stopping. During the planning phase a player can toggle between movement and activating a unit’s ability. If they choose ability, the opposing player is given a chance to move their units out of the way (if applicable)unles.

Elimination is won by defeating the opposing fireteam. In King of the Hill, the six central cells form a control zone; a team wins by holding sole control at the end of three consecutive rounds. Units eliminated while their team still has survivors respawn at their assigned deployment cell before the next round without regaining ability charges. Eliminating the entire opposing fireteam still wins immediately.

# Clash Mini / Clash Royale ability-VFX reference

Mood board and quality bar for the Battle Plan ability-VFX pass.

All frames were extracted from gameplay video at source resolution. Ability moments were located automatically by detecting transient spikes in the fraction of near-white pixels (a VFX flash), rejecting any spike that straddled an editing cut, then pulling an aligned triplet at **-0.28s / peak / +0.38s**. That is why `windup/`, `impact/` and `aftermath/` files that share a name are three moments of the *same* cast and can be compared directly.

Every `source URL` is deep-linked to the second the frame was taken from.

Frames were rejected if they were under 400px on the short edge, blurred pillarbox padding, menu or loading screens, card art, title cards, commentary captions, or near-duplicates of a frame already kept.
